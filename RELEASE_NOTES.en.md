## What's new in v1.7.0

- **Two languages:** Bulgarian and English – chosen at the first start, changed in Settings → System → Language.
- **The picture's story:** where it is, a small map, “Show on the map” and “Read on Wikipedia”.
- **Archive and search:** the 🔍 button at the top – every picture you saw plus older ones from NASA and Wikimedia, search by word, category, colour, country and year.
- **Art break:** every 20 pictures you browse, a famous painting and its story; it can become your wallpaper.
- **Unlock and double tap:** optional – a new picture every time you wake the phone, or with a double tap on the home screen.
- **Albums:** optional – sort your photos into albums and choose which album goes on which screen.
- **By time, season and weather:** optional – some pictures in the morning, others in the evening; snow in winter, sea in summer, cosy ones when it rains.

**Install:** on the phone tap the file `DnevenTapet-….apk` below (under “Assets”) and open it.
